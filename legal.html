Investment definitions

Legal and market knowledge

JSE Limited operates a securities exchange where companies seeking capital and investors seeking market opportunities can meet. BP Bernstein's current site identifies the firm as a JSE member for Equities and Authorised JSE Investments.

Shares are units of ownership in a company. Shareholders participate in the company's value and may receive distributions when declared, while accepting that market prices and investment values can rise or fall.

Investing places capital into assets with the aim of producing income or growth over time. Outcomes are not guaranteed, and appropriate decisions depend on an investor's circumstances, objectives and tolerance for risk.

An exchange-traded fund generally holds a portfolio designed around a stated strategy or index and trades on an exchange. Objectives, holdings, costs and risks differ between funds and should be reviewed before investing.

An exchange-traded note is a listed debt security whose return is linked to an underlying security or benchmark. The investor is exposed to the movement of that reference and to the credit risk of the issuing institution.
