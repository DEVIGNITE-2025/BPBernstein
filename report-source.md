**Audience:** BP Bernstein website project team  
**Prepared:** 6 September 2026  
**Scope:** Company identity, regulated status, services, account structures, account-opening information and safe website content.

## Executive answer

BP Bernstein (Pty) Ltd is listed by the JSE as an **Equities Member** (alpha code **BPB**; registration number **2005/044092/07**) at Unit 7, 1 Parade on Kloof, Oriel, Bedfordview. Its official legacy website describes a stockbroking and tailored-portfolio service, and lists Equity Trading, CFD Trading, Offshore Investments, ETNs, ETFs, Satrix and Structured Products. Its official account-opening materials publish detailed documentation checklists for South African individuals, non-resident individuals, minors, trusts, (Pty) Ltd companies, close corporations and clubs/non-legal entities.

The new site has been updated to make those supportable account checklists selectable. It deliberately does **not** publish banking instructions, fee scales, investment performance or claims about current product partners. Those either create a security risk, are time-sensitive, or could not be confirmed as current.

## Account-opening requirements placed in the site

The account selector on `open-account.html` now uses the detailed requirements below. These are transcribed and condensed from BP Bernstein’s public mandate pages. They should still be confirmed with the account-opening team before a customer relies on them, because the current mandate pack controls.

### SA individual

- Initialled and signed mandate
- Certified South African ID
- Certified proof of residence not older than three months
- Certified bank statement or bank letter confirming banking details
- SARS-issued document confirming tax number

### Non-resident SA individual

- Initialled and signed mandate
- Certified passport
- Certified proof of residence not older than three months
- Certified bank statement or bank letter confirming banking details
- Foreign income-tax document confirming tax number, where applicable

### Minor

- Mandate in the minor’s name, signed by the guardian in that capacity
- Certified birth certificate
- Certified guardian ID and proof of residence not older than three months
- Declaration that the minor lives with the guardian
- Certified bank statement or bank letter
- SARS document confirming the parent/guardian tax number

### Trust

- Trustee resolution signed by all trustees and signed mandate
- Certified IDs and proof of residence for trustees and beneficiaries
- Proof of trust registered address, trust deed and Master of the High Court authorisation
- Trust will and named beneficiaries
- Certified trust bank statement/bank letter and SARS tax-number document

### Pty (Ltd)

- Board resolution naming the authorised director and signed mandate
- Certified CEO/director IDs and address verification as described in the BPB checklist
- Registered address records, directors list, incorporation certificate and company letterhead
- Certified company banking confirmation and SARS tax-number document

### Close corporation

- Member resolution naming the authorised member and signed mandate
- Certified ID/address verification of authorised person
- CK1/CK2, physical-address evidence and CC letterhead
- Banking confirmation and SARS tax-number document

### Club/non-legal entity

- Resolution signed by beneficiaries/members and signed mandate
- Certified identification for beneficiaries/members and address verification for nominated person
- Formation agreement and nominated-account banking confirmation
- SARS document for the nominated person’s tax number

## Known gaps — do not invent these

- **Joint-account and foreign-company checklists:** BP Bernstein lists both structures but no complete public requirements page could be retrieved. The new site correctly directs customers to request the current pack.
- **Current fee schedule, minimum funding and transaction charges:** legacy pages contain fee material, but the versions are time-sensitive and inconsistent across indexed copies. Do not publish them until BP Bernstein supplies an approved current schedule.
- **Structured products and offshore product partners:** the legacy site refers to these, but public material does not confirm current availability or partners. Use “subject to availability” and request confirmation.
- **Banking details:** not reproduced, even where a legacy page exposed them. Customers should receive payment instructions through a verified BP Bernstein channel.
- **FSP scope/current authorisation:** the old BPB site states FSP licence 40810 for CFDs and offshore investments. Verify the current scope in the FSCA register before making the exact scope a prominent live claim.

## Recommendations for publication

1. Ask BP Bernstein’s compliance/account-opening team to approve each online checklist and provide signed current mandate PDFs.
2. Add current limits, fees, turnaround expectations and document certification rules only after written approval.
3. Keep the “speak to an adviser” path visible for joint and foreign-company accounts.
4. Add a routine owner and review date to the account-requirements content; this information is regulatory and can change.
