bpbernstein Member of the JSE

Established nearly 60 years ago, BP Bernstein is a member of the JSE Limited and one of the oldest independent stockbroking firms in South Africa.

Since the early 1950’s both private investors and institutions have relied on BP Bernstein to help them identify and capitalise on financial opportunities.

BP Bernstein is a JSE member for Equities and Authorised JSE Investments, and an authorised Financial Services Provider (FSP 40810) for CFDs and offshore investments.
